# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Ilo kaj Kialo Uploader is a private tool used only by the Ilo kaj Kialo channel to upload its own animated videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

## What we access

When the channel owner authorizes the app with TikTok, the app receives basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar) and permission to upload videos to the account as drafts. We do not access other users' data, messages, followers or analytics.

## How we store it

The access token is stored only on the channel's private server, readable by a single system account, and is used only to upload the channel's own videos. It is never sold, shared or sent to third parties.

## Deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. To ask us to delete stored data, email ilokajkialo@gmail.com.
